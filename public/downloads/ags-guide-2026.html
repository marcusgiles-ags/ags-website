08 / Insider Essentials

Technical Preparation for the Tropical Round.

Packing Logic

Humidity is the variable. Focus on moisture-wicking technical fabrics. We recommend bringing two pairs of footwear to rotate between rounds. AGS can coordinate high-spec rental sets (TaylorMade/Callaway) if you prefer to travel light.

Caddy Etiquette

The caddies in SE Asia are exceptional. They aren't just bag carriers; they are your local field tactical advisors. Trust their green reading — they know the grain better than anyone. Standard tipping is expected and we provide you with a localized guide for each country.

Customs & Fast Track

Every AGS tour includes VIP Airport Fast Track. Skip the queues in Da Nang, Bangkok, or Phnom Penh. Your Guardian will meet you at the gate and navigate the bureaucracy while you focus on the journey ahead.
