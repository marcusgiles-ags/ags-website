Guest access
Guests enjoy full and private access to the entire villa and all facilities during their stay. This includes all bedrooms, indoor and outdoor living areas, kitchen, private swimming pool, garden, and direct beachfront access.

The villa is exclusively booked for one group at a time, making it ideal for families or groups of friends looking for privacy, space, and a relaxed holiday atmosphere. No areas are shared with other guests.

Our team will only enter the property for housekeeping or maintenance at agreed times to ensure guests’ comfort and privacy.

Other things to note
The villa is located in a peaceful beachfront area at the end of a small laneway in An Bang, offering a quiet and relaxing environment while still being within walking distance of beach restaurants, cafés, and local shops.

The property is especially suitable for families and groups seeking a calm holiday setting rather than a party venue. We kindly ask guests to respect the neighborhood and keep noise levels reasonable, particularly in the evening.

Being a beachfront property, guests may occasionally experience natural coastal conditions such as wind, sea sounds, or sand carried by the breeze — all part of the authentic seaside experience.

Airport transfers, BBQ or afternoon tea services, daily housekeeping, and local experience recommendations can be arranged before or during your stay to make your holiday as comfortable as possible.
