BEN villa – beachfront villa in An Bang
This eco-friendly villa features unique architecture with 3 bedrooms, 3 bathrooms, a private pool, a practical kitchen with the essentials for everyday cooking, a dining area, and a spacious living room. With its open-space design, BEN seamlessly connects indoors and outdoors, letting in natural light and fresh sea breeze. Step into a secret garden, listen to the sound of waves, enjoy ocean views from your room, and leave all the chaos behind the gate.

The space
Ben Villa is a beachfront villa designed with an open-space concept, featuring recycled materials and reclaimed wood, giving it a rustic yet elegant charm.

Step out of the gate and the white sandy beach stretches right before your eyes.

All rooms offer ocean or garden views.

A local market is just a 3-minute walk away, and there are plenty of bars and restaurants nearby.

The living room is designed in an open-space layout without air conditioning, while all bedrooms are equipped with air conditioning to ensure maximum comfort during your stay

Guest access
You are welcome to use all facilities and appliances in the house, except for items stored in the storage room.

Other things to note
Please note: Due to the tropical climate and open-space architecture, small insects such as ants, mosquitoes, or geckos may occasionally appear. These creatures are harmless and very common in Vietnam. However, we follow a regular cleaning and pest-control routine to ensure the space remains clean and comfortable for our guests.
