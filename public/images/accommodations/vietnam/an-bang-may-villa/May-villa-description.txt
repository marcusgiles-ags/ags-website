MAY VILLA - a 6 bedrooms beachfront villa at An Bang, Hoi An

Surrounded by a friendly and quiet fishing village, the house itself has a private pool and a big green lawn garden. You can get private access to the beach from the back of the property.

Follow the pathway from the house will take you to an outdoor sitting place where you can find BBQ set up or a sun lounge for these hot summer days.

The space
Two twin blocks in the house feature 6 bedrooms, 6 bathrooms, 2 kitchens, and 2 living rooms overlooking the swimming pool. All rooms offer ocean and garden views, with attached modern bathrooms.

Guest access
Guests are entitled to use all the amenities available in the house.
