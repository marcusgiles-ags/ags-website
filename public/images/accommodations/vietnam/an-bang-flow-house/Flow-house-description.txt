Located in the heart of An Bang, Flow House is a beautiful beachfront villa ideal for families and groups.

The house offers 3 comfortable bedrooms, a fully equipped kitchen, spacious living room with floor-to-ceiling windows, and many seating areas to enjoy sea breezes.

Guests can relax in the green backyard, experience a tranquil setting with ocean views, and explore nearby restaurants, beach bars, and a local market just 5 minutes’ walk away in a peaceful fishing village.

The space
- The living room and kitchen: with open space architecture, they are located on the first floor without air condition. You can enjoy the wonderful view of the garden in front of the beach.
- The 1st floor bedroom: with a large bed, balcony with garden view.
- The 2nd floor bedrooms: there are 2 bedrooms on 2nd floor, one bedroom has a large bed, with a balcony you can see the sea. The other with 2 twin beds.
All bedrooms are equipped with air condition.

Other things to note
Please note: Due to the tropical climate and open-space architecture, small insects such as ants, mosquitoes, or geckos may occasionally appear. These creatures are harmless and very common in Vietnam. However, we follow a regular cleaning and pest-control routine to ensure the space remains clean and comfortable for our guests.
