Located in the beautiful village of An Bang in Hoi An, our beachfront SALT VILLA is the perfect accommodation choice for a group of friends or multi-generational family. With 3 bedrooms and 3 bathrooms, it sleeps up to 6 people. In addition to endless ocean view, Salt offers an outdoor swimming pool and many seating areas. Boasting comfortable beds, western amenities and attention to detail, you are guaranteed to have everything you need for a beach vacation and more.

The space
The villa over looks the beach and you can choose to pull up a beach chair on the beach or use the house’s private swimming pool.

Guest access
Whole property is yours.

Other things to note
We can arrange for a private car to pick you up from the Da Nang International Airport or train station. The cost is USD$15 for a 4 seat car, $17 for 7 seats or $26 for a 16 seat van. Please let us know if you would like us to arrange this for you as the price is actually cheaper than if you were to take a taxi. All we require is your flight or train details.

If you like to cook and need help getting groceries, please send us a message and our staff will be more than happy to assist. They can arrange to either take you grocery shopping or take your shopping list and go buy the items for you.
