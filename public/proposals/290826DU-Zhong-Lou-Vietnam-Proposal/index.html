Ranking

Location

Designed by the "Great White Shark" Greg Norman, The Bluffs Grand Ho Tram is arguably Vietnam's most celebrated golf course, set along a rugged coastal strip where dramatic towering sand dunes meet the crashing waves of the East Sea. Norman described it as one of the most spectacular natural pieces of land he has ever had the privilege to work with.

With massive elevation changes of up to 50 metres, wind that constantly shifts off the ocean, and fast-running fairways carved through native sand dunes, The Bluffs offers a true Scottish-style links test in tropical warmth. Playing it twice (Tee times: 12:56 on Day 4 and 08:06 on Day 5) allows you to fully appreciate Norman's strategic genius and master shot selection from different tee angles and wind directions.
