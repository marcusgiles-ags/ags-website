Round 1 • Day 2

Laguna Lăng Cô Golf Club

Sir Nick Faldo's masterwork — where mountains meet the sea

~1 hr from Hoi An • Lăng Cô Bay

Six-time major champion Sir Nick Faldo's Laguna Lăng Cô Golf Club is one of the most visually dramatic courses in all of Southeast Asia — and among the most technically impressive. Situated at the heart of Vietnam's first fully integrated luxury resort on the shores of Lăng Cô Bay, the course occupies a stretch of land of exceptional natural beauty: the Hai Van Pass rises sharply to the north, Lăng Cô's turquoise lagoon stretches to the south, and the course threads between rice paddies, jungle waterways, and undulating terrain to create a layout of genuine variety and strategic interest.

At just under 7,100 yards from the back tees and 5,263 yards from the front, Faldo has successfully designed a course that challenges the scratch player while remaining thoroughly accessible to the higher handicapper — a technical achievement that many architects fail to deliver. The signature hole is the short par-4 9th, widely considered one of the best par 4s in Vietnam. Running directly parallel to the pristine Lăng Cô beach, it offers unobstructed panoramic views of the East Sea. Coastal winds can significantly impact club selection when trying to find both tight fairway landing zones and when approaching the green.

Round 2 • Day 4

Ba Na Hills Golf Club

Asia's finest mountain golf — where altitude meets artistry

Par / Yardage
Par 72 / 7,858 yds
Designer
Luke Donald (with IMG)
Established
2016
Location
~45 min from Hoi An • Foothills

The first design on Asian soil from former World No. 1 Luke Donald, Ba Na Hills Golf Club has established itself as one of the most visually spectacular golf experiences in Southeast Asia. Set against the imposing backdrop of the Ba Na Hills mountain range — which rises steeply from the coastal plains just 25 kilometres west of Da Nang — the course occupies a landscape of exceptional natural drama, with deep ravines, towering hardwood canopy, and mountain streams that define the character of numerous holes.

At 7,858 yards from the championship tees, the layout is one of Vietnam's longest, though the yardage somewhat overstates the challenge compared to a traditional flat parkland. The clever use of elevation change, both in terms of topography and psychological effect, is Donald's masterstroke here. The front nine plays through a more open, parkland-influenced terrain with long, sweeping fairways before the back nine ascends into the hills proper, delivering a succession of blind shots, doglegs, and dramatic par-3s with panoramic views across the Ba Na valley floor.

Particular highlights include the par-3 12th hole — a magnificent downhill shot to a green protected by a natural stream — and the demanding par-5 5th, which requires three precise shots to reach a narrowly guarded green. The comprehensive clubhouse and driving range are managed to IMG's international standard, and the floodlit course offers the novel experience of 18 holes after dark if the group's ambition extends that far into the evening.

Vinpearl Golf Nam Hoi An

Coastal dunescape artistry — casuarina pines and ocean breezes

Par / Yardage
Par 72 / 7,038 yds
Designer
IMG Worldwide
Established

Location

Designed by global architectural authority IMG Worldwide, Vinpearl Golf Nam Hoi An is an 18-hole championship masterpiece situated within a prestigious 200-hectare seaside eco-resort complex just 20 minutes south of Hoi An Ancient Town. Flowing seamlessly through an unspoiled coastal terrain of expansive white sand dunes, seasonal wetland reserves, and mature casuarina pines, the course provides an intimate, visually striking connection with Central Vietnam's indigenous coastal environment.

The par-72, 7,038-yard championship layout is celebrated for its wide, generous fairways that invite confident driving, yet subtly conceals strategic layers of complexity for low handicappers. Massive, naturalized waste bunkers flank landing zones, while prevailing sea breezes off the East Sea add a dynamic, shifting variable to every club choice. The course features Platinum Paspalum turf from tees to greens, providing a velvety, firm, and true ball roll. Elevation changes across the dune ridges provide sweeping ocean horizon views without ever creating unfair or punishing lies.

The signature hole is undoubtedly the sweeping par-5 9th hole, which winds dramatically across a natural dune amphitheatre back toward the clubhouse, accompanied by the stunning par-3 16th playing across a tranquil native lake surrounded by whispering casuarinas. Complemented by an ultra-modern clubhouse, luxury locker rooms, a pro shop stocked with premier equipment, and dedicated PGA-standard driving facilities, Vinpearl Golf Nam Hoi An delivers an effortless, five-star resort golf experience of the highest calibre.

Round 4 • Day 7 (Grand Finale)

Montgomerie Links Golf Club

Colin Montgomerie's links masterwork — Scottish soul on Vietnam's Central Coast

Par / Yardage
Par 72 / 7,090 yds
Designer
Colin Montgomerie
Established
2010
Location
~20 min from Hoi An • Coast

Created by eight-time European Tour Order of Merit winner and Ryder Cup legend Colin Montgomerie, Montgomerie Links Vietnam carries the unmistakable DNA of traditional European links golf, brilliantly transplanted to the warm sands of the Central Coast. Widely recognized as the pioneer layout that established Da Nang and Hoi An as an elite international golf destination, the course is routed through a natural landscape of wind-swept coastal dunes, indigenous casuarina pine forests, and sandy waste hazards just hundreds of metres from the East Sea.

At 7,090 yards from the championship tees, Montgomerie's design philosophy prioritizes strategy, course management, and precision over sheer brute distance. Firm, fast-running fairways and expansive waste sand hazards demand that players carefully position their tee shots to open up receptive approach angles into complex, heavily contoured greens. Sculpted pot bunkers with deep sod-like revetments penalize offline approaches, mirroring the classic links courses of Scotland and Ireland while playing across manicured SeaIsle 2000 paspalum turf that ensures pristine year-round playing surfaces.

Standout highlights include the signature par-5 12th hole, a visually arresting dogleg wrapped around towering pines and water hazards, and the dramatic par-5 18th that climbs gently toward the iconic, multi-tiered clubhouse with its panoramic terrace overlooking the closing holes. Recognized multiple times as Vietnam's Best Golf Course and rated 5-stars by international golf authorities, Montgomerie Links pairs an exhilarating on-course test with comprehensive PGA-grade practice facilities, putting greens, and one of the finest clubhouse dining experiences in Southeast Asia.

Golf Protocol & Dress Code

  • Attire: Collared polo shirts and tailored golf shorts or trousers required. Denim and cargo pants are strictly prohibited.
  • Spikes: Soft spikes only across all four championship courses.
  • Caddies: Individual caddies are allocated to every golfer. They read greens, calculate distances, and manage equipment.
  • Tipping: Standard caddie gratuity is approximately 500,000 VND (~$30 AUD) per round, handed directly to your caddie post-round in cash.
☀️

February Climate & Packing

  • Golden Window: February is one of the absolute best months to visit Central Vietnam! Clear sunny skies, minimal rain, and comfortable temperatures averaging 22°C to 27°C.
  • Essentials: High-SPF sunscreen, UV arm sleeves, wide-brim golf hat, and light breathable clothing.
  • Footwear: Comfortable walking shoes or sandals for Old Town cobblestones.
  • Evenings: A light jacket or windbreaker is useful for pleasant rooftop breezes.
