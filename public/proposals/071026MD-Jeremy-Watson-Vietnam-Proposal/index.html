08 - Know Before You Go

Destination Information

Hoi An & Central Vietnam - Everything you need to know

⛳

Why Golf Here?

Central Vietnam's Da Nang-Hoi An corridor is unambiguously Asia's premier golf destination. Within a 60-minute radius of Hoi An, your group has access to more than 12 championship layouts - including multiple entries on Golf Digest's Top 100 Courses in the World list. The concentration of world-class architecture (Norman, Donald, Faldo, RTJ Jr., Nicklaus) in such a compact geography is genuinely unmatched anywhere in Asia.

Early May is firmly within Central Vietnam's golden window - warm, clear mornings ideal for golf, with lower humidity than peak summer and manageable afternoon temperatures. Water conditions are excellent at all courses in this period.

✈

  • Most Australian travellers route via Singapore (SIN), Kuala Lumpur (KUL), or Ho Chi Minh City (SGN)

Tipping Guidelines

  • Caddies: VND 500,000 (~USD $20) per caddie per round is the recommended tip - your caddies are assigned individually, carry your bag for 18 holes in the heat, and are genuinely integral to your score. Tip them well in cash at the end of the round.
  • Drivers/Transfers: USD $3-$5 per trip or USD $10-$15 per day for a full-day driver is appropriate.
  • Hotel Porter/Bellboy: 20,000-50,000 VND per instance.
  • Restaurants: 5-10% if service charge not included - check the bill.
  • General: Tipping is not mandatory in Vietnam but is genuinely valued. Cash in Vietnamese Dong or USD is equally acceptable.
🏌

What to Pack

  • Attire: Collared golf shirts and tailored shorts/trousers required at all courses. No denim. No sleeveless shirts at most venues.
  • Footwear: Soft spikes required. Some courses have complimentary shoe cleaning services post-round.
  • Climate: Early May is warm and mostly sunny - temperatures average 28-32°C with moderate humidity. A lightweight rain jacket is recommended. Vietnam's championship layouts drain well and play resumes quickly after showers.
  • Sun protection: SPF 50+ minimum, UV arm sleeves, and a wide-brim golf hat are strongly recommended - you're close to the Equator.
  • Golf equipment: Rental clubs available at all courses. Bag storage and golf locker rooms at each venue.
🍜

Must-Try Local Dishes

Cao Lầu
Hoi An's signature noodle dish - thick rice noodles with pork, bean sprouts, and herb greens, served with minimal broth. The noodles are traditionally made with water drawn from an ancient Cham well in the Old Town. You cannot authentically eat this dish anywhere else in the world.
Mì Quảng
Central Vietnam's defining noodle soup - wide, turmeric-stained rice noodles with a small amount of rich broth, topped with pork, shrimp, toasted sesame rice crackers, and fresh herbs. A profound counterargument to anyone who thinks Vietnamese cuisine is only about pho.
Bánh Mì Phượng
Anthony Bourdain called Bánh Mì Phượng's version the "best sandwich in the world." A baguette of extraordinary crispness, filled with pâté, pickled daikon, coriander, and your choice of protein. A 10-minute walk from the Old Town - essential.
🌐

General Travel Info

  • Currency: Vietnamese Dong (VND). AUD 1 ≈ 16,000 VND (verify current rate). Major hotels accept USD and credit cards.
  • Electricity: 220V / 50Hz. Two-pin round plugs standard. Australian 3-pin adapters may not fit - bring a universal adapter.
  • Language: Vietnamese. English is widely spoken at all golf courses and 5-star hotels.
  • Time Zone: ICT (UTC+7). No daylight saving. Australian EST is 4 hours ahead (3 hours in AEST summer).
  • Connectivity: Excellent 4G/LTE coverage throughout Da Nang and Hoi An. Most hotels offer strong WiFi. Recommend local SIM card for AUD $10-15 on arrival - available at the airport.
  • Safety: Da Nang and Hoi An are among Vietnam's safest tourist destinations. Standard precautions apply.
⚕

Health & Wellbeing

  • Vaccinations: Consult your GP 6-8 weeks before departure. Hepatitis A, Typhoid, and a current Tetanus are commonly recommended for Vietnam.
  • Water: Do not drink tap water. Bottled water is provided in all hotel rooms and on golf transfers.
  • Mosquitoes: Low risk in early May in both Da Nang and Hoi An, but DEET-based repellent is recommended for evening activities in the Old Town.
  • Medical: Vietnam Family Medical Practice in Da Nang is the go-to English-language clinic. Our AGS 24/7 support line can assist with any health-related logistics.
  • Travel Insurance: Compulsory - comprehensive medical evacuation coverage is strongly recommended.
