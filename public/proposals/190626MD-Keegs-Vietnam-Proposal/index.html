03 — The Layouts

Golf Courses

Four championship tracks. Four distinct design philosophies. One unforgettable week.

Round 1 — Day 2

Montgomerie Links Vietnam

Where European precision meets the Vietnamese coastline

Par / Yardage
Par 72 / 7,081 yds
Designer
Colin Montgomerie
Established
2009
Location
~20 min from Hoi An Old Town

Designed by eight-time European Tour Order of Merit champion Colin Montgomerie, Montgomerie Links Golf Club occupies a pristine stretch of land between the Truong Giang River and the South China Sea — a setting that provides both visual drama and genuine strategic challenge. The layout is widely regarded as one of Central Vietnam's finest parkland-style tracks, with a sand-based construction that delivers firm, fast conditions throughout the year and an exceptional surface underfoot regardless of the season.

The course rewards thought over brute force. Montgomerie's signature style — generous landing areas off the tee that funnel into precisely defended approach zones — is on full display here. The greens are large, multi-tiered and nuanced; putts from the wrong quarter of the green can be brutally unforgiving. The closing stretch along the back nine, where the river comes into play alongside the coastal breeze, is as engaging a finish as you'll encounter anywhere in the region.

The championship facilities are commensurate with the course's reputation: a world-class driving range, well-appointed locker rooms, and a clubhouse that handles a group of 12 without missing a beat. The caddies at Montgomerie are experienced, bilingual where required, and excellent club selectors in coastal wind conditions — listen to them on the approach shots and you'll score better for it.

Round 2 — Day 3

Ba Na Hills Golf Club

Asia's finest mountain golf — where altitude meets artistry

Par / Yardage
Par 72 / 7,858 yds
Designer
Luke Donald (with IMG)
Established
2016
Location
~45 min from Hoi An · 25 min from Da Nang

The first design on Asian soil from former World No. 1 Luke Donald, Ba Na Hills Golf Club has established itself as one of the most visually spectacular golf experiences in Southeast Asia. Set against the imposing backdrop of the Ba Na Hills mountain range — which rises steeply from the coastal plains just 25 kilometres west of Da Nang — the course occupies a landscape of exceptional natural drama, with deep ravines, towering hardwood canopy, and mountain streams that define the character of numerous holes.

At 7,858 yards from the championship tees, the layout is one of Vietnam's longest, though the yardage somewhat overstates the challenge compared to a traditional flat parkland. The clever use of elevation change, both in terms of topography and psychological effect, is Donald's masterstroke here. The front nine plays through a more open, parkland-influenced terrain with long, sweeping fairways before the back nine ascends into the hills proper, delivering a succession of blind shots, doglegs, and dramatic par-3s with panoramic views across the Ba Na valley floor.

Particular highlights include the par-3 12th hole — a magnificent downhill shot to a green protected by a natural stream — and the demanding par-5 5th, which requires three precise shots to reach a narrowly guarded green. The comprehensive clubhouse and driving range are managed to IMG's international standard, and the floodlit course offers the novel experience of 18 holes after dark if the group's ambition extends that far into the evening.

Round 3 — Day 5

BRG Legends Golf Resort — Nicklaus Course

The Bear's most demanding Vietnamese design

Par / Yardage
Par 72 / 7,254 yds
Designer
Jack Nicklaus
Established
2009
Location
~15 min from Da Nang Airport

The Jack Nicklaus-designed layout at BRG Legends Golf Resort holds a singular distinction in Vietnamese golf: it was the first course in the country to deploy bulkheads in its design — a structural engineering and aesthetic decision that gives the course its immediately recognisable character. These raised wooden retaining walls not only define the perimeter of water hazards but alter the strategic calculus on numerous holes, demanding precision off the tee rather than simply power through the air.

Make no mistake — this is a golf course designed to test professionals, and it does exactly that. BRG Legends regularly hosts events on the Asian Development Tour, and the course conditions maintained for those events are the same standard your group will experience. Water hazards are not decorative; they're actively in play on a majority of holes across both nines. The layout rewards patience and strategic withdrawal — the golfer who accepts a par on the water-guarded par-4 seventh will score better than the one who fires at the flag and discovers the carry is three clubs more than it looks.

Nicklaus's fingerprints are evident throughout: bold, sweeping bunker complexes positioned to catch the ambitious line, green complexes with subtle false fronts, and par-5s designed to reward the genuinely long hitter while offering a layup route for the strategically inclined. The finishing hole — a dramatic par-4 with water guarding the approach — has ended many a stableford card. Worth every conversation it generates.

Round 4 — Day 6 · Finale

Hoiana Shores Golf Club

World's Best New Golf Course 2020 · Cham Islands views · True links golf

Par / Yardage
Par 71 / 7,053 yds
Designer
Robert Trent Jones Jr.
Established
2020
Location
~30 min from Hoi An · Coastal setting

There is no course on your itinerary — and very few in all of Southeast Asia — that can match Hoiana Shores for pure visual drama. Robert Trent Jones Jr.'s links masterpiece occupies a finger of duneland between the Eastern Sea and the Thu Bon River estuary, with the Cham Islands visible on the horizon from multiple vantage points. The World Golf Awards named it the World's Best New Golf Course in 2020, and it remains one of the defining layouts of the modern Asian golf boom — a layout that feels ancient and timeless despite being less than a decade old.

True links golf in the classical sense: the sea wind is not incidental but structural. Club selection is rarely straightforward because the breeze shifts in intensity and direction throughout the round. The natural duneland topography provides rollicking fairways with significant contour — the bump-and-run is not only valid but often the optimal approach, particularly on the exposed oceanside holes of the front nine. Several greens are elevated above their surroundings, offering panoramic views across the South China Sea that are genuinely distracting in the most welcome possible way.

Architect Jones Jr. has created an exceptional par-3 sequence — including the dramatic 6th, playing into the prevailing ocean wind with a green elevated above the dunegrass — that rivals any short-hole collection in the region. The finishing holes, returning toward the luxurious clubhouse along the river estuary, complete a round that demands strategic cohesion from the first tee to the final green. An extraordinary way to close out a week of exceptional golf in Central Vietnam.

05 — Know Before You Go

Destination Information

Da Nang & Hoi An, Central Vietnam — Everything you need to know

⛳

Why Golf Here?

Central Vietnam's Da Nang–Hoi An corridor is unambiguously Asia's premier golf destination. Within a 45-minute radius of Hoi An, your group has access to more than 12 championship layouts — including multiple entries on Golf Digest's Top 100 Courses in the World list. The concentration of world-class architecture (Nicklaus, Donald, Montgomery, RTJ Jr., Greg Norman) in such a compact geography is genuinely unmatched anywhere in Asia.

October is firmly within Central Vietnam's "golden window" — the pre-typhoon season months of September and October deliver warm, mostly clear mornings ideal for golf, with afternoon cloud cover providing relief from the heat. Water conditions are excellent at all courses in this period.

Tipping Guidelines

  • Caddies: VND 500,000 (~USD $20) per caddie per round is the recommended tip — your caddies are assigned individually, carry your bag for 18 holes in the heat, and are genuinely integral to your score. Tip them well in cash at the end of the round.

  • Hotel Porter/Bellboy: 20,000–50,000 VND per instance.
  • Restaurants: 5–10% if service charge not included — check the bill.
  • General: Tipping is not mandatory in Vietnam but is genuinely valued. Cash in Vietnamese Dong or USD is equally acceptable.
🏌

What to Pack

  • Attire: Collared golf shirts and tailored shorts/trousers required at all courses. No denim. No sleeveless shirts at most venues.
  • Footwear: Soft spikes required. Some courses have complementary shoe cleaning services post-round.
  • Climate: Mid-October is usually the start of the wet season when storms start rolling in off the East Sea. Temperatures average 28–32°C with occasional afternoon cloud and lower humidity than peak summer — but pack accordingly. A lightweight rain jacket is essential, not optional. You will very likely experience rain on the golf course at some point during the week. The good news: Vietnam's championship layouts drain exceptionally well and play resumes quickly after showers.
  • Sun protection: SPF 50+ minimum, UV arm sleeves, and a wide-brim golf hat are strongly recommended — you're close to the Equator.
  • Golf equipment: Rental clubs available at all courses. Bag storage and golf locker rooms at each venue.
🍜

Must-Try Local Dishes

Cao Lầu
Hoi An's signature noodle dish — thick rice noodles with pork, bean sprouts, and herb greens, served with minimal broth. The noodles are traditionally made with water drawn from an ancient Cham well in the Old Town. You cannot authentically eat this dish anywhere else in the world.
Mì Quảng
Central Vietnam's defining noodle soup — wide, turmeric-stained rice noodles with a small amount of rich broth, topped with pork, shrimp, toasted sesame rice crackers, and fresh herbs. A profound counterargument to anyone who thinks Vietnamese cuisine is only about pho.
Bánh Mì Phượng
Anthony Bourdain called Bánh Mì Phượng's version the "best sandwich in the world," and the original bakery is a 10-minute walk from the Anantara. A baguette of extraordinary crispness, filled with pâté, pickled daikon, coriander, and your choice of protein. Essential.
🌐

General Travel Info

  • Currency: Vietnamese Dong (VND). AUD 1 ≈ 16,000 VND (verify current rate). Major hotels accept USD and credit cards.
  • Electricity: 220V / 50Hz. Two-pin round plugs standard. Australian 3-pin adapters may not fit — bring a universal adapter.
  • Language: Vietnamese. English is widely spoken at all golf courses and 4/5-star hotels. Some working knowledge of French exists at older properties.
  • Time Zone: ICT (UTC+7). No daylight saving. Australian EST is 4 hours ahead (3 hours in AEST summer).
  • Connectivity: Excellent 4G/LTE coverage throughout Da Nang and Hoi An. Most hotels offer strong WiFi. Recommend local SIM card for AUD $10-15 on arrival — available at the airport.
  • Safety: Da Nang and Hoi An are among Vietnam's safest tourist destinations. Standard precautions apply — watch your belongings in crowded market areas.
⚕

Health & Wellbeing

  • Vaccinations: Consult your GP 6-8 weeks before departure. Hepatitis A, Typhoid, and a current Tetanus are commonly recommended for Vietnam.
  • Water: Do not drink tap water. Bottled water is provided in all hotel rooms and on golf transfers.
  • Mosquitoes: Low risk in October in both Da Nang and Hoi An, but DEET-based repellent is recommended for evening activities in the Old Town.
  • Medical: Vietnam Family Medical Practice in Da Nang is the go-to English-language clinic. Our AGS 24/7 support line can assist with any health-related logistics.
  • Travel Insurance: Compulsory — comprehensive medical evacuation coverage is strongly recommended.
