Introduction

Our Brand Standards

This document defines the brand identity standards for Asia Golf Specialists. It establishes our strategic positioning, visual identity, verbal framework, and the rules that govern how we present ourselves across every touchpoint—from digital platforms to physical collateral.

Consistent application of these guidelines builds brand equity, earns trust, and ensures that every interaction with AGS communicates the same message: uncompromising excellence in Southeast Asian golf.

01

Brand Strategy

Purpose, Positioning & Promise

1.1 — Our Mission

The Purpose

To curate and execute the most uncompromising, elite golf touring experiences in Southeast Asia for those who demand championship standards.

We exist at the intersection of high-end property curation and professional golf logistics. AGS does not just book trips—we architect elite escapes. Every detail, from tee-time sequencing to villa selection, is orchestrated with the precision of a championship event.

What We Guarantee

“You focus on your game. We focus on everything else.”

This is the contract between AGS and our clients. We remove the weight of coordination, providing a seamless framework that allows the group to focus entirely on the game and shared company. Our promise is total elimination of travel friction.

Brand Voice

The “Aussie Wit” Protocol

2.1 — Overview

How We Speak

AGS speaks to the high-net-worth Australian golfer. The voice must be Direct, Masculine, and Sophisticated—with a distinctly Australian undercurrent.

We are warm and conversational but never casual. We speak with the confidence of someone who has played every course, stayed in every villa, and negotiated every transfer. Our authority is earned, not declared.

We adapt our tone depending on the channel. Social media allows for more playful wit; the website demands editorial precision; itineraries require clinical clarity. The underlying voice—confident, insider, premium—remains constant.

Personality

Conversational but authoritative. We speak with confidence, providing reassurance to our clients while being witty and light-hearted when the moment calls for it. Our attitude is empathetic, engaged, and distinctly Australian.

Language

A mix of short, punchy directives and longer, evocative descriptions. Our style is informal but never sloppy. We use subtle Australian colloquialisms—“sorted,” “track,” “layout”—woven naturally into premium copy.

Feeling

The reader should feel they’re speaking with a trusted mate who happens to be an elite concierge. Not a corporation. Not a chatbot. A person who genuinely wants them to have the trip of a lifetime.

2.3 — Copywriting Standards

Do’s & Don’ts

✓ Do

  • Speak in directives: “Assemble the mates.”
  • Flex technical knowledge: mention course designers, agronomy, local conditions
  • Use subtle Aussie colloquialisms: “All good,” “track,” “layout,” “sorted”
  • Prioritise clarity and brevity
  • Use active voice exclusively
  • Lead with benefits, not features
  • Reference specific course architects by name

✗ Don’t

  • Use cheap marketing jargon: “Unlock,” “Synergy,” “Solutions”
  • Beg for the sale or use desperate urgency
  • Use passive voice
  • Be generic: “5-star hotel” or “world-class experience”
  • Over-explain or use filler words
  • Use emojis in formal communications
  • Compete on price or mention discounts
2.4 — Tone Across Channels

Adapting Our Voice

Website & Itineraries

Editorial precision. Authoritative and evocative. Long-form descriptions paint the scene; technical details build trust. The website is our flagship—every word must earn its place.

Social Media

More playful and conversational. Room for Aussie wit and punchy one-liners. Visual-first, with copy that supports rather than leads. Hashtags are functional, not decorative.

Email & Direct Communication

Personal and warm. First-person “we” preferred. Sign-offs are human: “See you on the fairway.” We write like a trusted friend with insider access, not a sales funnel.

3.2 — The Erawan Monogram

The Club Mark

The Erawan—the three-headed elephant of Southeast Asian mythology—is our brand symbol. It represents wisdom, strength, and the interconnection of our three pillars: golf, travel, and cultural immersion. The crossed golf clubs and ball complete the “Club Mark” variant, which is our only approved monogram.

Design recommendation: The filled silhouette version is the primary monogram. It delivers the strongest visual impact at any scale, reproduces cleanly in single-colour applications (embroidery, debossing, digital favicons), and maintains legibility from billboard to golf ball. The outline variant is the secondary option for contexts requiring a lighter, more detailed aesthetic—such as stationery or editorial layouts.

3.3 — Exclusion Zone

Clear Space

The exclusion zone ensures legibility and impact. It provides the minimum safe distance from competing visual elements. The measurement unit “X” equals the diameter of the golf ball within the Club Mark.

  • Logo: 2X clear space on all sides
  • Monogram only: 1.5X clear space on all sides

Typography

5.1 — Primary Typeface

Playfair Display

Our primary typeface is Playfair Display, a transitional serif with high contrast and distinctive proportions. It reinforces our “Old Money” aesthetic—conservative, architectural, and unmistakably premium. Playfair Display should be used for all headings, brand statements, and display typography.

Aa Bb Cc Dd Ee Ff Gg Hh

ABCDEFGHIJKLMNOPQRSTUVWXYZ

abcdefghijklmnopqrstuvwxyz 0123456789

Playfair Display • Weights: 400 (Regular), 500 (Medium), 600 (SemiBold), 700 (Bold), 800 (ExtraBold) • Italic available

5.2 — Secondary Typeface

Montserrat

Montserrat is our workhorse sans-serif. Clean, masculine, and highly legible, it handles body text, navigation, UI labels, and all functional typography. Its geometric structure complements Playfair’s classical elegance without competing.

Aa Bb Cc Dd Ee Ff Gg Hh

ABCDEFGHIJKLMNOPQRSTUVWXYZ

Montserrat • Weights: 300, 400, 500, 600, 700

5.3 — Type Hierarchy

Scale & Usage

H1 Headline

H2 Section Title

Playfair Display SemiBold • 28–36px • Line-height: 1.2

H3 Subsection

Playfair Display Medium • 20–24px • Line-height: 1.3

SECTION LABEL / TAG

Montserrat SemiBold • 10–12px • Uppercase • Letter-spacing: 2–3px

Body text uses Montserrat at regular weight for optimal readability across all screen sizes and print formats.

Montserrat Regular • 14–16px • Line-height: 1.7–1.8

5.4 — Typography Rules

Standards & Misuse

Navigation & Labels

All navigation items, tags, and UI labels use Montserrat in ALL CAPS with 2px letter-spacing. Weight: 500–600.

Kerning & Tracking

Headings: optical kerning, 0 tracking. Body: auto kerning. Uppercase labels: +2px tracking minimum. Never compress letter-spacing below 0.

Forbidden

Never use decorative or script fonts. Never stretch or compress type. Never set body text in Playfair Display. Never use light weights below 14px.

Photography & Imagery

Visual standards for all brand photography

6.1 — Photography Standards

Visual DNA

All AGS photography must feel like a high-end travel editorial. We do not use stock photography that looks generic or staged. Every image should make the viewer want to be there.

Lighting

Golden hour or sunrise exclusively. Long shadows, high contrast, warm tones. Midday harsh light is never acceptable for hero or editorial imagery.

Perspective

Master-shot drones for course aerials. Eye-level cinematic for interiors and lifestyle. Low-angle for architectural grandeur. Never Dutch angles or extreme fisheye.

Technical Specs

Shot on 35mm equivalent or wider. High contrast with deep navy shadows. Ultra-realistic textures—no filters, no HDR processing. Colour-graded to match the brand palette.

6.2 — Image Categories

What We Photograph

Championship Courses — The primary image category. Courses should appear pristine, empty, and inviting.

Accommodation & Lifestyle — Luxury without ostentation

The Game — Equipment, course details, the ritual of golf

6.3 — Image Treatment

Overlays & Integration

Hero Overlays

When text appears over photography, use a directional gradient overlay:

  rgba(2,6,23, 0.85))

Image Rules

  • Never crop logos or text into photography
  • Maintain aspect ratios—never stretch
  • Use object-fit: cover for responsive containers
  • Colour grade to warm tones; avoid cool/blue casts
  • People in images should be incidental, never posed

Digital Architecture

7.1 — Design Philosophy

Heavy & Premium

The AGS digital experience must feel “heavy” and premium. Quality is conveyed through massive breathing room, slow deliberate interactions, and an absence of visual clutter. Every pixel earns its place.

Massive Breathing Room

Section padding of 12rem forces the user to digest one monumental thought at a time. White space is a luxury signal.

padding: 12rem 0;

The Card System

Solid navy backgrounds with thin gold borders. On hover, translate −8px vertically, border shifts to solid gold, apply a warm glow shadow.

border: 1px solid
rgba(212,175,55, 0.2);

The Naked CTA

Primary buttons are “ghosted”—transparent backgrounds, gold borders, uppercase Montserrat. Never solid-fill buttons.

EXPLORE TOURS
7.2 — The Signature Interaction

Cinematic Motion

All hover effects, card transitions, and page animations use a luxurious, cinematic easing curve. Fast interactions feel cheap; slow, deliberate motion feels premium.

/* The AGS Signature Curve */
transition: all 0.8s cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1);

When the brand leaves the screen, it must retain its “Old World” weight. Every physical touchpoint communicates quality through material and finish.

Merchandise

  • Navy on navy, or gold on navy

  • Club Mark monogram: left chest or back of collar

The Welcome Pack

  • Navy leather scorecard holder
  • Debossed Erawan Club Mark
  • Custom ball markers in Ochre Gold
  • Presented upon arrival, never mailed

8.3 — Social Media

Platform Guidelines

Profile Images

Use the filled Club Mark monogram on a Midnight Navy background. Never use the full lockup—it becomes illegible at avatar sizes. Maintain square aspect ratio.

Content Imagery

Course photography dominates the feed. Maintain a 3:1 ratio of photography to designed graphics. Never use stock photography with watermarks or visible compression artefacts.

Branded Graphics

Designed posts use the Midnight Navy background with Playfair Display headlines and gold accents. Logo placement: top-right corner with exclusion zone respected.

8.4 — Website

Digital Flagship

The website at asiagolfspecialists.com is the definitive expression of the AGS brand. It serves as the reference implementation for all digital guidelines described in this document. Key patterns:

Page Structure

  • Full-bleed hero with gradient overlay
  • Gold tag → Large serif H1 → Subtle subtext
  • 12rem section spacing
  • Card-based content with gold borders
  • Ghost CTA buttons throughout

Itinerary PDFs

  • Delivered via dark-mode app or high-aesthetic PDF
  • 50/50 split: storytelling left, visuals right
  • Day markers with gold numbering
  • Floating image cards with deep shadows
  • Gold captions in Montserrat uppercase
