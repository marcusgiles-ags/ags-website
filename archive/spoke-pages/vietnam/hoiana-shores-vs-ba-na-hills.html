Hoiana Shores vs. Ba Na Hills

The Dunes vs. The Peaks.

Robert Trent Jones Jr.'s debut in Vietnam is a masterpiece of the "Ground Game" — a stark contrast to the dramatic elevations of Ba Na Hills.

Technical Dominance: Coastal Sand vs. Mountain Zoysia

Hoiana Shores is about the "Ground Game" — firm fairways where the ball runs and a cerebral challenge against the coastal breeze. Ba Na Hills is "The Big Show" — dramatic vistas, lush green fairways, and the unique experience of playing in a cool mountain valley.

The X-Factor: Night Golf and the "Serpent's Finale"

Ba Na Hills offers a full floodlit "Night Golf" experience, transforming the mountain layout into a high-energy arena. Hoiana Shores offers the "Serpent's Finale" (Holes 15-18), a tactical gauntlet among shifting dunes.

The Local Guardian's Verdict: Technical Acclimatisation

We typically start groups at Ba Na Hills to acclimate to the mountain air, then move to the coast for the pure links finale at Hoiana. The back nine at Ba Na is far more demanding — save your energy and trust your caddy's local knowledge.
