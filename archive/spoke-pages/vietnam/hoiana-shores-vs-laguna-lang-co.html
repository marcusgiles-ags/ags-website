Hoiana Shores vs. Laguna Lang Co

The Links vs. The Tropical Faldo Masterpiece.

Sir Nick Faldo has woven a course through rice paddies and jungle outcrops at Laguna Lang Co — a stark contrast to the open dunes of Hoiana Shores.

Technical Dominance: Ground Game vs. Aerial Attack

Hoiana Shores is an intellectual links challenge — firm-and-fast fairways that reward the traditional "Ground Game." Laguna Lang Co is "Target Golf" where the scenery changes every three holes, and you must hit your spots or face a long day in the tropical flora.

The Journey: Dunes vs. Jungle

Hoiana is about the game; Laguna is about the journey. The transitions at Laguna take you through mountain tunnels and over bridges. While Hoiana is a walking-style experience, Laguna is cart-centric with scenic transitions through jungle, rice paddies, and sea.

The Local Guardian's Verdict: Tactical Sequencing

We recommend playing Laguna mid-tour as the "scenic" confidence-builder before finishing with the high-stakes atmosphere of Hoiana Shores. Laguna's Zoysia fairways give a generous "sitting up" lie before the challenging coastal dunes.
