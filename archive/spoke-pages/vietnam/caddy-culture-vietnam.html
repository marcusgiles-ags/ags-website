Caddy Culture: The Local Guardian

Navigating the Solo Game with a Grain-Aware Expert.

An experienced caddy providing a technical line on the undulating greens. In Vietnam, your caddy is more than a bag-carrier — they are your tactical advisor.

Technical Dominance: Beyond the GPS

Don't fight the local expertise — embrace it. In Vietnam, relying solely on GPS or lasers is a technical error. A senior caddy understands the "Grain" of the TifEagle greens and predicts the swirling mountain/sea crosswinds that a device simply cannot measure.

The Tipping Truth: Supporting the Local Economy

While the caddy fee is included in your AGS package, tipping is the local standard and a vital part of the regional golf economy. We recommend a tip of VND 400,000–500,000 (~$17–$21 USD) for an 18-hole round.

The Local Guardian's Verdict: Cross-Cultural Camaraderie

Most senior caddies at our partner courses speak functional "Golf English." Fostering cross-cultural camaraderie is part of the "Member for a Week" philosophy. Your caddy isn't just there to carry clubs — they're there to help you blow the "Horn of Plenty" at the 18th.
