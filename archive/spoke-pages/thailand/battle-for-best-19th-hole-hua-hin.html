The Battle for the
Best 19th Hole in Hua Hin

The sun dipping behind the Burmese mountains as seen from the terrace at Banyan Golf Club. In Hua Hin, the "Honor Roll" is decided and the "Horn of Plenty" is blown.

Technical Dominance: The Architecture of the Aftermath

In Hua Hin, the battle for dominance doesn't end on the 18th green. We believe the quality of the 19th hole is a technical requirement for a legacy tour. Whether it's the traditional Lanna elegance of Mulligan's Pub at Banyan or the high-energy, open-air "Stadium Hub" at Black Mountain, your choice of terrace defines the social energy of your group.

The Ritual: Sunset and Leaderboards

Choose Banyan (Pineapple Valley) if you value camaraderie and reflection. It is arguably the best clubhouse in Asia for a post-round drink — watching the sun set over the Tanowsri mountains while the sea breeze cools the terrace is a mandatory AGS experience. Choose Black Mountain if you want to stay in the competitive flow, watching the final groups finish while analyzing live leaderboard data on the deck.

The Local Guardian's Verdict: The Gala Finale

We often suggest starting your tour's social life at Black Mountain to feed off the energy, but we always save the final "Honor Roll" gala for the Banyan terrace. There is simply no better place in the region to watch the bets get settled. Ask your Local Guardian which clubhouse is currently hosting the most exclusive sunset events for your dates.
