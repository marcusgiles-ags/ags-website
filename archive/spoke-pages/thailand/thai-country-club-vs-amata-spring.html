Thai Country Club vs. Amata Spring

The Duel of the Elite in Bangkok's private circuit.

A choice between royal treatment and a tour-level beast. Both are private. Both are exceptional. Only one has a floating green.

Technical Dominance: Hospitality vs. The Floating Green

Thai Country Club is the choice for royalty — a prestigious atmosphere where world-class hospitality and perfectly manicured fair tests of golf are the standard. Amata Spring is an invitational beast designed for the high-stakes "Executive Sprint." It is "Target Golf" at its finest, culminating in the legendary boat ride to the floating 17th green.

The Pitch: Paspalum vs. Zoysia

The technical difference is in the turf. Thai Country Club's Paspalum fairways are lush and dense. Amata Spring's Zoysia fairways are firm and upright, requiring precision ball-striking. Most golfers leave Thai Country Club talking about the legendary locker room service; they leave Amata Spring talking about the floating green.
