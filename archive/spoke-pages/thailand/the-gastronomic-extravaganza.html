Technical Dominance: From Northern Lanna to Southern Spice

We believe a "Member for a Week" should eat like a local legend. In the North, this means the herbal, sophisticated flavors of Lanna culture — slow-cooked pork and river fish in teak-clad heritage mansions. In the South, it's the "Southern Coastal" intensity — wild-caught shellfish and the legendary Moo Hong (braised pork belly) in open-air shacks or high-end beachfront estates.

The Ritual: Settle the Bets with Local Mastery
