The Rise of Phnom Penh

Asia's New Luxury Nightlife Capital.

The cantilevered views from Sora Sky Bar at the Rosewood, overlooking a capital in the midst of a luxury transformation.

Technical Dominance: High-Altitude Mixology

For the refined traveler, Phnom Penh in 2026 is a sanctuary of sophistication. Start your evening at Sora — cantilevered over the Mekong — for elite mixology and 360-degree city views. For those who value reflection, the Sora Whisky Bar offers rare single malts and a climate-controlled humidor.

The Clandestine Wave: Hidden Gems for the Insider

The true pulse of the city is found in its speakeasies. Venues like Battbong, hidden behind a vintage vending machine, prioritize privacy and artisanal spirits infused with local botanicals. These are the spaces of the business elite — far removed from the mainstream tourist track.
