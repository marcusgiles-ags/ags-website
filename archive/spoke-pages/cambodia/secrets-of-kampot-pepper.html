Black Gold: Secrets of Kampot Pepper

The Gourmet Adventure of Cambodia's "Protected" Spice.

The legendary "Black Gold" of Cambodia. This PGI-protected spice adds a rich, sensory layer to your Cambodian residency.

Technical Dominance: From Flower Heat to Sweet Aromatics

Understanding Kampot Pepper is a technical requirement for the gourmet traveler. The variety depends on harvest timing: Green for floral, citrusy herbals; Black for robust, pungent heat; Red for sweet, floral notes; and White for clean, smooth elegance.

The Gourmet Respite: A Coastal Sensory Journey

A day trip to the pepper estates of Kampot provides a welcome respite from the fairways. We arrange sensory tours where you can taste the spice at the source — from pepper-infused gins to artisanal chocolates. A narrative layer to your tour, connecting you with the "Black Gold" of the Kingdom.

The Local Guardian's Verdict: Gourmet Protocol

Taste the world's finest spice at the source. We coordinate "Gourmet & Greens" day trips including private transfers to the most prestigious estates and beach-side seafood lunches where the pepper takes center stage.
