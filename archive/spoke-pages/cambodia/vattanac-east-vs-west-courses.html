Vattanac: East Course vs. West Course

The Cultural Grandeur vs. The Technical Precision.

The architectural duality of Vattanac Golf Resort — Cambodia's premier 36-hole destination in Phnom Penh.

Technical Dominance: Cultural Grandeur vs. Technical Precision

The East Course (Heritage) is defined by cultural iconography, featuring scale models of Angkor Wat and the Bayon. It is a course of grand scale and expansive mounding. The West Course (The Specialist) offers a more technical, secluded feel, defined by Dragon Lake — narrow, tree-lined fairways that reward the "Shot-Maker."

The Signature Experience: GPS Precision and VIP Facilities

In 2026, Vattanac boasts one of the largest and most luxurious clubhouses in Southeast Asia. The VIP Wing offers private dining suites and a pro shop stocked with exclusive European brands. GPS-equipped carts are mandatory, providing precise yardages to the "hidden" pin positions that the resort is famous for.

The Local Guardian's Verdict: The Visionary vs. The Tactician

Choose East if you want to be wowed by historical tributes at scale. Choose West if you want to test your mettle on a technical, intimate layout where a "Green in Regulation" is a hard-earned victory. Most tours include both to experience the full duality.
