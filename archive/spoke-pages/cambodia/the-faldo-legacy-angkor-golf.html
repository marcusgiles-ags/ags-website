The Faldo Legacy: Angkor Golf Resort

Why It Is Cambodia's Finest Golf Masterpiece.

Sir Nick Faldo's celebrated layout, where every hole tells a story of strategic elegance. Playing Angkor Golf Resort is a non-negotiable for the serious SEA golf traveler.

Technical Dominance: The Faldo Architecture School

Sir Nick Faldo is the master of penal design — courses that demand precision over power. Angkor Golf Resort's redesigned layout is no exception. Featuring water hazards that test your decision-making from the tee, strategically angled bunkers that punish the lazy line, and Paspalum greens that reward an aggressive but controlled approach.

The Signature Experience: Cultural Iconography Meets Golf

What sets Angkor apart from other layouts in the region is the visual narrative. The ancient stone gods of the Bayon statue line the fairways, the club house draws from Khmer architectural heritage, and the morning mist rolls across the layout from the nearby Tonle Sap — creating a playing environment that is genuinely unrepeatable.

The Local Guardian's Verdict: Cambodia's Non-Negotiable

Angkor Golf Resort is the strongest argument for including Cambodia on any serious Asia golf itinerary. The Faldo challenge, the cultural backdrop, and the world-class service combine to deliver a genuinely unique masterpiece. Most of our Cambodia tours play here twice — once to learn it, once to conquer it.
